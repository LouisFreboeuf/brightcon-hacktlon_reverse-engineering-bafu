S3 · the report names the inputs, the optimiser sets the amounts

Althaus et al. (2007), ecoinvent report No. 8, p. 365 (PDF p. 446), Tab. 44.3 · specs/e6293140-anthraquinone-at-plant.draft.json

Anthraquinone is the case that exposed our labelling problem. The table is not the dataset's inventory. It is a literature table from Vogel 2000 covering four synthesis routes, and only the chromic acid column has numbers. BAFU's dataset is an eighty-twenty average of that route and confidential EMPA data that is not printed, so even the chemicals cover only eighty percent of the story. Then the energy paragraph underneath: the report names an electricity mix, a steam dataset, a chemical plant and standard transport distances, and gives an amount for none of them. Those became free parameters, and the fit pushed four of the five to their lower bound, so in the rebuilt process steam, infrastructure and transport are effectively zero. The input list is the report's. The amounts are the optimiser's. That is S3, and until this week our pipeline stamped it S1.
