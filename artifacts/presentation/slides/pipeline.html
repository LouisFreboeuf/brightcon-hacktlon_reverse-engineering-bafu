Before the details, the whole path in one picture. Step one, find: every dataset BAFU flags as a system process, plus the ones that give themselves away by having no production input at all; that is 138. Step two, read: we locate the report the dataset cites and extract what it says about inputs and amounts, or, where no report helps, we look for a unit process of the same product elsewhere in BAFU. What we find decides the route, S1, S2 or S3, which is the next slide. Step three, specify: we write down the input list, map every input to a real BAFU dataset, and for every amount record the sentence it came from; an amount the evidence does not give is left open. Step four, fit: the open amounts are solved for, so that the supply chain of the rebuilt process reproduces the aggregated flows of the original, all eighteen hundred of them at once. The small picture is exactly that: the known inventories of the inputs, times the unknown amounts, should equal the known aggregated vector. Step five, build and check: the unit process is written into BAFU, with its inputs linked to real datasets, and a residual block is added for whatever it does not explain, so the hybrid reproduces the original exactly. Then we compare flow by flow and export it. In: one aggregated dataset and its documentation. Out: a unit process you can open, edit and trace, that still gives the same answer.
