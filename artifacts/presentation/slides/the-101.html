Source: results/system_terminated.csv · 101 datasets flagged type=2 in the BAFU‑2026 ecoSpold export

The first job was just counting. 101 datasets in BAFU-2026 are shipped as system processes. We joined every one to its cited source and to the 114 report PDFs in the documentation bundle. Only 34 have a report we can actually read. The 67 without documentation are not a failure of method — PlasticsEurope states explicitly that its data is nowadays only available as system processes. Note the uncomfortable part: the family with the biggest downstream impact, PlasticsEurope polymers, is the one with no public data. Impact and feasibility are anti-correlated here.
