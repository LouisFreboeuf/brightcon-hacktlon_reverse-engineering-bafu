These are the three we took all the way through. Burnt shale is the clean case: the treeze concrete report prints the whole gate-to-gate table, so it is close to pure transcription, and the rebuilt unit process carries essentially all of the system process's mass. Cement ZN/D is the interesting one for the optimiser: the composition is given as ranges, so the six shares were fitted inside those bounds with a one-kilogram mass constraint. Gypsum fibre board is the honest failure — the German inventory is confidential, we reused the structure of the Swiss unit process, and the fit tells us clearly that the German plant is more energy-intensive than that structure allows. That is a useful result too: the method told us it did not work, rather than hiding it.
