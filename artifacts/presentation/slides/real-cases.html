Eleven taken all the way through. The top three are the ones worth dwelling on. Burnt shale is the clean case: the treeze concrete report prints the whole gate-to-gate table, so it is close to pure transcription, and the rebuilt unit process carries essentially all of the system process's mass. Cement ZN/D is the interesting one for the optimiser: the composition is given as ranges, so the six shares were fitted inside those bounds with a one-kilogram mass constraint. The fourth row is the honest one. For eight datasets no report documents them at all, but BAFU still ships a unit process of the same product, so we transferred that structure and calibrated. They all build, and their hybrids reproduce the originals exactly, but only two to seven percent of the individual flows land within ten percent. The reason is that these aggregated datasets are older vintages whose supply chains no longer match today's unit processes, and the fit ends up pinned against its bounds. Same name, different plant. That is a result, not a failure to report.
