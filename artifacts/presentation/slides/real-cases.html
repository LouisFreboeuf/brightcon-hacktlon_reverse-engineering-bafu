Fifty-one of the hundred and thirty-eight, taken all the way through, and the split is the first point. Only one is a true transcription, burnt shale, where the report prints every amount. Eleven reuse the structure of a unit process BAFU already has for the same product. Thirty-nine, more than three quarters, are top-down models: the evidence names the inputs, not every amount. The plot on the right is every elementary flow of all fifty-one rebuilds against the original, about eighty-five thousand of them. They follow the line, but loosely: of the flows both sides have, eighteen percent match within ten percent and forty-two percent within a factor of two, and the largest flows match no better than the trace flows. Fossil carbon dioxide, the flow most people look at, is within ten percent for thirteen of the fifty-one, and the top-down models run about eighteen percent low, most likely because the fit pushes the energy inputs the reports never quantify towards zero. Part of the gap is not ours: the originals were computed on older background data, and even BAFU's own dis-aggregated epoxy misses its aggregate's fossil carbon dioxide by a factor of two point seven. Two boxes at the bottom. Twenty-two of the fifty-one are datasets the ecoSpold flag never told us about. And nineteen still terminate on an aggregate we did not reach; eight datasets cause all of it, and Ethylene, average, at plant alone blocks nine.
