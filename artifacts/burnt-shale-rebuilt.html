Strategy used

  1. Evidence: report transcription S1Tab. 3.9 of Ökobilanz ausgewählter Betonsorten (Tschümperlin, Ramseier, Frischknecht 2020) lists the inventory per tonne with the 71.3 % economic‑allocation column (electricity co‑product takes 28.7 %). Tab. 3.10 gives transport distances. Litres of diesel and heating oil were converted at 36 MJ/l.
  2. Terminate on the database resolveEach line item was mapped to an existing BAFU unit process by name and location — blasting, diesel in building machines, DE medium‑voltage grid, light fuel oil furnace, tap water, HCl, NaOH, hypochlorite, organic chemicals, rail and lorry transport, sewage treatment. All 12 resolved; none is itself aggregated, so no new nodes and no dependency ordering.
  3. Direct flows from the target vectorTwo resource flows no upstream node can emit — 0.96 kg raw shale and 3.66 MJ crude‑oil resource (the ecoinvent convention for the shale's own energy content) — were taken from the original vector. This is legitimate exactly because they are direct: they cannot be an artefact of the supply chain.
  4. Calibrate one amount, list fixed NNLSOnly grid electricity was marked free. The fit against the original vector (1,398 flows, fitted in relative error with each (unit, compartment) group carrying the same total weight — no characterisation) put it at 0.14 kWh/t instead of the table's 68.6 kWh/t — the plant co‑generates 183.8 kWh/t, so the original model nets its own generation. A convention the report does not state; the harness found it.
  5. Build explicit + hybrid S5The explicit node is the model. The hybrid adds the residual as direct flows so its cumulative inventory equals the original; the residual's share per category measures how much is still unexplained.

What is still missing

The energy‑driven categories agree. The small‑score categories do not, and the flow diff says why — each points at a flow the table does not print:

Water use +146 %

  • Original emits 0.0108 m³ water to water and draws 0.0103 m³ turbine water — a water balance the "unpolluted wastewater" route does not reproduce.

Ecotoxicity, freshwater (organics) −93 %

  • Aromatic hydrocarbons and PAH to water in the original — crude‑oil handling in a fuel chain modelled differently from the ones linked here.

Toxicity, land use, ozone −35 … −70 %

  • Metals from shale burning, land occupation of the quarry, and halogenated flows — none in Tab. 3.9; candidates for the next iteration with the flow diff as the guide.

Structural checks

  • ✓12 explicit inputs over 1 node — plausible for a quarry‑plus‑kiln process
  • ✓no decomposed grid mixes (the free fit had replaced the grid with individual power plants)
  • ✓no dependency on another aggregated dataset
  • ✓mass in: 0.57 kg technosphere + 0.96 kg resources per kg product — consistent with 1.35 kg raw shale × 71.3 % allocation
  • ✓all inputs and flows resolved to existing BAFU / EF 3.1 objects

Why these matter: with a free input list, least squares reproduced all 25 scores of a known unit process to 1.000 while choosing 116 wrong inputs. Score agreement is necessary, not sufficient.
