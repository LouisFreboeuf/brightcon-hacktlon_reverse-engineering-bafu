**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model: the two components the dataset's name and technology field state, split fitted under a 1 kg mass constraint. NOT S1. No report in the bundle prints an inventory. The dataset's ecoSpold technology field states the composition qualitatively - 'production out of hexamethylene diamine and adipic acid, filled with glass fibers' - and both components exist in BAFU: the unfilled polymer as an unflagged eco-profile and 'Glass fibre, at plant' [RER] as a real 20-input unit process. Compounding is a physical blend, so polymer + glass = 1.000 kg per kg of compound, which is imposed with mass_sum; the glass content is nowhere quantified in the bundle (commercial grades run 15-50 %), so the split is fitted, not measured, and is flagged low confidence. Note the circularity BAFU already contains and this spec inherits: 'Glass fibre, at plant' itself consumes 0.0283 kg of 'Nylon 6, at plant' as a sizing agent, so a glass-filled nylon depends on nylon twice.

AFTER THE FIT - READ THIS BEFORE USING THE AMOUNTS. The fit put 0.9995 kg on the polymer and 2.3E-4 kg on the glass fibre, i.e. it modelled a glass-*filled* compound as unfilled polymer; commercial grades carry 15-50 % glass. The inventory agreement it buys is real (601 of 1669 determined flows within +-10 % (36 %), 20 of the 50 largest kilogram flows, 89 % of the kilogram mass, median |delta| 12.2 %) and the node is a fair reconstruction of the target's flows, but its composition is not a statement about the product and must not be quoted. The mechanism is the same one recorded on the ABS, SAN and HIPS specs: under a mass constraint the fit empties whichever candidate is an ecoinvent unit process - here 'Glass fibre, at plant', a 20-input dataset that drags a full ~1670-flow background into a target whose eco-profile reports ~145 substances - and keeps the candidate that belongs to the same APME family and therefore shares the target's waste-treatment chains. No bound was moved after the score was seen.
