# Check: Epoxy resin, liquid, at plant (c80b8e9e-8a0d-36f6-a4a2-1509d03e42c2)

target `bafu-2026` vs explicit model `c80b8e9e-8a0d-36f6-a4a2-1509d03e42c2-disagg` and hybrid `c80b8e9e-8a0d-36f6-a4a2-1509d03e42c2-hybrid`

**Strategy:** S2 — template transfer: BAFU-2026 ships the disaggregated version of this very dataset. The one case in the whole set where the answer is already inside the database. Chapter 31 of 2007 - LCI chemicals - Althaus.pdf prints two inventories of liquid epoxy resin side by side: Tab. 31.1, 'Life cycle inventory ... based on cumulative data', which is the target, and Tab. 31.2, 'Disaggregated life cycle inventory for the production of liquid epoxy resin (modified from Boustead 1999)', which is the same product as a unit process - 9 technosphere exchanges plus its own resources and direct emissions. BAFU-2026 carries both: the target, and 'Epoxy resin, liquid, disaggregated data, at plant' [RER] (code 6ac7f2be, 9 inputs, 63 elementary flows), whose exchanges reproduce Tab. 31.2 line for line - checked one by one: 2.19 kWh electricity, 11.0 MJ heavy fuel oil, 57.7 MJ natural-gas furnace, 13.0 kg tap water, 0.298 and 0.0058 kg of the two disposal services, 4.0E-10 chemical plant, 1.74 tkm rail, 60.6 tkm lorry. So this spec links that node 1:1 and calibrates nothing. A parallel spec, specs/c80b8e9e-epoxy-resin-liquid-at-plant.draft.json, transcribes Tab. 31.2 through the LLM route instead; the two should agree, and where they do not the difference is transcription or flow mapping, which is worth knowing. What the check measures here is not how good this project's method is - it is how far BAFU's own disaggregated dataset lands from BAFU's own aggregated one for the same product, which is the cleanest available answer to 'what does disaggregation cost?'. One thing to expect: the two BAFU datasets put their air emissions in different sub-compartments - the aggregated one in 'Emissions to urban air close to ground', the disaggregated one in 'Emissions to air, unspecified' - so a substance total that is exactly right can still score as two large deviations.

AFTER THE CHECK. The result is the most useful number this batch produced, because it is a control with no modelling in it at all: BAFU's own disaggregated epoxy resin against BAFU's own aggregated epoxy resin, 1 kg for 1 kg. On the substances that carry the dataset it is close - fossil CO2 +1.5 %, rock salt +3.6 %, chloride to water +11.2 %, sodium to water +11.8 %, suspended solids -13.7 %, calcium +18.4 %, calcite +53.2 % - and it covers 75 % of the target's kilogram mass. On the project's headline metric it scores 6 of 1669 determined flows within +-10 %, i.e. 0 %. Both numbers are correct and they measure different things: the second is dominated by ~1500 trace flows that exist only because the two datasets reach different backgrounds, and by the sub-compartment split between them (the aggregated dataset puts its air emissions in 'Emissions to urban air close to ground', the disaggregated one in 'Emissions to air, unspecified', so a substance total that is exactly right scores as two large deviations). Anyone reading a low 'share of flows within 10 %' for this family should compare it with this row first.
