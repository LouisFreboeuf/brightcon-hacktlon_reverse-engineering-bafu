**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model: the two components the dataset's name and technology field state, split fitted under a 1 kg mass constraint. NOT S1. No report in the bundle prints an inventory. The dataset's ecoSpold technology field states the composition qualitatively - 'production by different ways out of caprolactam and filled with glass fibers' - and both components exist in BAFU: the unfilled polymer as an unflagged eco-profile and 'Glass fibre, at plant' [RER] as a real 20-input unit process. Compounding is a physical blend, so polymer + glass = 1.000 kg per kg of compound, which is imposed with mass_sum; the glass content is nowhere quantified in the bundle (commercial grades run 15-50 %), so the split is fitted, not measured, and is flagged low confidence. Note the circularity BAFU already contains and this spec inherits: 'Glass fibre, at plant' itself consumes 0.0283 kg of 'Nylon 6, at plant' as a sizing agent, so a glass-filled nylon depends on nylon twice.

AFTER THE FIT - READ THIS BEFORE USING THE AMOUNTS. The fit put 0.99999 kg on the polymer and 4E-6 kg on the glass fibre, i.e. it modelled a glass-*filled* compound as unfilled polymer; commercial grades carry 15-50 % glass.
